Whip is a touch-friendly Android client for supervising Herdr agents and working on remote development hosts over SSH.

Connect directly to machines you control or reach private destinations through saved jump hosts. Whip verifies SSH host keys and keeps credentials protected by Android Keystore.

Features include:

* Monitor agents from multiple Herdr hosts in one attention queue
* Prompt agents with a native multiline composer
* Attach to full terminal sessions with mobile-friendly controls
* Browse, upload, download, edit, and preview remote files over SFTP
* Open local and private web services through on-demand SSH tunnels
* Generate and manage SSH keys, jump hosts, and agent forwarding
* Receive local notifications when agents become blocked or finish
* Customize themes, backgrounds, terminal fonts, gestures, and controls

Whip connects Android directly to your SSH host. It does not use a Whip-operated relay and does not include advertising or analytics.

The F-Droid build keeps credential recovery on the device and does not include Google Play Services. Google Play builds can additionally use Android Block Store for encrypted credential recovery.

Whip is not developed, maintained, or endorsed by the Herdr project or its authors.
