# HyperFrames production brief

- Product: Whip, a native Android companion for Herdr.
- Audience: developers already using coding agents on remote machines.
- Promise: supervise and interact with a whole Herdr workflow from Android over
  the user's own SSH connection.
- Runtime: 30 seconds.
- Master: 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC.
- Music: **Techno Fights** by Alejandro Magaña (A. M.), Mixkit Stock Music
  Free License; conformed from 140 BPM to the 128 BPM motion grid.
- Autoplay-safe: every claim is understandable without sound.
- Evidence: current Whip UI captures and official Herdr desktop assets occupy
  most of the runtime.

## Story beats

1. Land an oversized Whip icon, then join it to the Herdr icon to establish Whip as Herdr's mobile client.
2. Rotate into a real Pixel showing all configured Android hosts and their connection states.
3. Advance from Hosts into Herd, then connect laptop and phone as synchronized views of one live session.
4. Deliver a real Android Whip notification when an agent finishes.
5. Advance from Herd into Terminal as focus moves from the laptop to Android.
6. Advance from Terminal into Whip's real SFTP Remote Files interface.
7. Hold Remote Files in the closing tableau and land the brand promise: leave the desk, keep the flow.

## Reference-derived direction

- Reveal the product in the opening second instead of leading with a title card.
- Alternate full-device shots with close UI crops to create camera rhythm.
- Keep each scene to one short claim and place copy opposite the product.
- Land scene changes on the soundtrack beat and animate the product continuously.
- Reserve a distinct, minimal closing shot for the brand and release message.
- Use device rotation, masked color fields, match cuts, and oversized crops—not
  simple slide transitions.
- Reuse shapes across cuts: the whip loop becomes an orbit, the orbit becomes a
  sync path, and the sync path carries focus from desktop to mobile.
- Treat the phone as a dual-sided 3D object, not a skewed screenshot: begin on
  an oversized back plate, pass through the thin hardware edge near 90°, reveal
  the live screen, then scale and settle into the interface composition.
